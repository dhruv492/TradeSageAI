Model Architecture & Methodology

Dual-model direction prediction with SHAP explainability and zero black-box risk

TradeSage AI combines technical indicators (RSI, MACD, Volume Delta, SMA) with NLP sentiment scoring. Predictions are rendered via two parallel architectures: a Random Forest Classifier (tabular baseline) and a Shallow PyTorch LSTM (temporal sequence learner). When both models diverge on directional bias, an explicit risk warning is highlighted.
